Wallet style storybook

Five complete visual styles for the Wallet app. Each one defines the launcher icon, colours for light and dark mode, type, shapes and effects, the in-app icon set, every component the app uses, and five screens. All styles keep two rules from the design: cards keep their brand colours (except Mono Dot, which reduces them to a dot), and the code panel is always white so scanners read it.

Pixel

Material 3 Expressive, as on a Google Pixel in 2027.

  • Tonal surfaces instead of shadows: depth comes from colour steps.
  • Expressive shapes: pill buttons, a large squircle add button and 'cookie' shapes for highlights.
  • One accent family (violet) for the app; cards keep their brand colours.

StrengthsWeaknesses
  • The most native look on Android; Compose Material 3 already implements most components.
  • Optional dynamic colour can follow the wallpaper.
  • Lowest implementation effort.

One UI 9

Samsung Galaxy, as on One UI 9.

  • Viewing area on top, interaction area at the bottom: big title, actions within thumb reach.
  • Content in grouped rounded containers on a slightly darker page.
  • Depth and gloss on tiles and the icon, like One UI 7+ app icons; teal accent instead of Samsung blue.

Fit for Wallet

StrengthsWeaknesses
  • Matches the phone it is built for: same title area, lists and switches as Settings.
  • Thumb-friendly by design.
  • No fonts to bundle.
  • Samsung has no public component library, so components are custom.
  • On non-Samsung phones the system font changes the feel.

Effort: Medium. Risk: Low.

Mono Dot

Monochrome with dot-matrix type, inspired by Nothing OS.

  • Black, white and one red accent; brand colour only as a small dot.
  • Dot-matrix type for names, routes and times; monospace labels in capitals.
  • Thin outlines, no fills, no shadows.

Fit for Wallet

StrengthsWeaknesses
  • The most distinctive and calm look.
  • Excellent contrast in both modes.
  • Brand colours shrink to a dot, so cards are slower to recognise at a glance, which is the app's main job.

Liquid Glass

Frosted translucent surfaces over a soft aurora.

  • Surfaces are frosted glass: blur, a white edge highlight and a hint of the colour behind.
  • A soft aurora background (violet, pink, teal) gives the app its colour.
  • Tiles are glossy, lit from the top left.

Fit for Wallet

StrengthsWeaknesses
  • The most eye-catching option.
  • Brand colours glow rather than sit flat.
  • Blur costs GPU time and battery; scrolling must stay smooth on the A56.
  • Text over blurred colour can lose contrast; needs careful tuning.
  • Can look dated quickly as trends move.

Effort: High. Risk: Medium.

Bold Ticket

Neo-brutalist: thick outlines, hard shadows, loud colour.

  • Everything has a 2.5 px ink outline and a hard 4 px offset shadow.
  • Cream paper, coral and lime accents, black ink.
  • Tickets look like tickets: notches and perforations.
