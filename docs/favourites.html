Favourites

Recommendation: 1 and 4 together, with 5 as the fallback. A star marks a favourite; favourites always sit at the bottom of the grid, next to the thumb, and the same favourites fill the widget, the app-icon shortcuts and the Quick Settings tile. When you have starred nothing, the app uses your most-used cards instead, so it is useful from day one.

Today the app has “Keep at top”, which in the bottom-up grid now means “keep nearest the thumb”; every idea below replaces it.

1. Star favourites by the thumb

A star replaces “Keep at top”. Starred cards always fill the bottom rows of the grid, right above Up next, under a small FAVOURITES label; the rest of the grid sits above, sorted by use.

StrengthsWeaknesses
  • Simplest: one tap to star, no new screen.
  • Fits the bottom-up grid: favourites are the closest cards to the thumb.
  • Favourites scroll away when the grid is long.

Effort: Low.

2. Favourites rail

A row of small favourite tiles fixed just above Up next and the bottom bar. It stays put while the grid scrolls above it.

StrengthsWeaknesses
  • Favourites are one tap away even in a long, scrolled grid.
  • Small tiles; four or five fit before the rail scrolls.
  • Takes a fixed strip of the screen.

Effort: Medium.

3. Swipe-up favourites

Swipe up on the bottom bar (or tap the star in it) to raise a sheet of big favourite tiles; release on a card to open it.

StrengthsWeaknesses
  • Big targets, nothing permanently on screen.
  • Works the same from the grid and later from the code screen.
  • Hidden until you learn the gesture; a star button in the bar helps.

Effort: Medium.

4. Favourites outside the app

Favourites drive everything outside the app: the Favourite cards widget, the long-press shortcuts on the app icon and the Quick Settings tile when nothing is up next.

StrengthsWeaknesses
  • Opens a favourite without opening the app first.
  • Combines with any of the in-app ideas.
  • Shortcuts are capped at four by the launcher.

Effort: Low.

5. Automatic favourites

No stars: the app learns which cards you open on which days and times (gym on Monday morning, groceries on Saturday) and shows them as the bottom row, labelled with the habit.

StrengthsWeaknesses
  • Zero setup; adapts as habits change.
  • Habit labels explain why a card is there.
  • Needs a few weeks of use before it is good.
  • Less predictable than a star.

Effort: Medium.
